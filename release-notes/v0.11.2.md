# Simple Kiosk Player v0.11.2

Wake playback stability fix.

## Changes

- Fix a race observed during scheduled wake playback: Android wakes and the app shows the video view, but the TextureView Surface is not ready, so MediaPlayer never starts.
- Add short retries while waiting for the video Surface after wake.
- Recreate the TextureView if the old Surface does not return after repeated retries.

## Notes

This targets the case where logs show `Received wake playback intent` and `Playing video...` but no following `Video layout...` entry.
